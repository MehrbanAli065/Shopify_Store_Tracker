Methodology · how this was computed

  • 01Variant-level reading. 1,247 size × colour × location combinations, not aggregated to product.
  • 02Stockout correction. 312 variant-days of OOS removed from the denominator before computing demand-side STR.
  • 03Returns-adjusted. Net of size-specific return rates pulled from Loop. Avg net-of-returns: −14% gross.
  • 04Waitlist as signal. 1,840 Klaviyo back-in-stock signups read at variant level as forward-demand evidence.
  • 05Ad-data overlay. 90 days of Meta and Google spend joined to variant-level OOS timeline.
  • 06Confidence intervals. Every projected number carries a ±range; only ranges with <15% spread shown as "High" confidence.
  • 07Human review. Reviewed by a senior fashion category planner before delivery.
